Frequently Asked Questions

Is this legal advice?
No. LexiPrompt provides AI prompt engineering tools for legal professionals. Outputs must be reviewed by qualified counsel before use.
Which AI models can I use the prompts with?
All major models — Claude (Anthropic), GPT-4o (OpenAI), Gemini (Google), Harvey/GC AI, and any enterprise AI that accepts system prompts.
What does "100 free prompts" mean?
Each time you complete the intake form and generate a prompt, that counts as one. You can generate 100 prompts across any frameworks before needing a paid plan.
Do you store my API key?
Your Anthropic API key is stored in your browser's localStorage only — it is never sent to LexiPrompt servers. Prompts are generated directly between your browser and Anthropic's API.
